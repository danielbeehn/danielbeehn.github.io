About

Nice to meet you

I am Daniel Beehn, a multidisciplinary designer based in central Illinois with a strong passion for creative problem-solving. I am committed to continuous growth, actively staying current with industry trends and expanding my skill set to deliver high-quality, effective design solutions.

With over 10 years of professional experience, I have collaborated with clients of all sizes on a wide range of projects, including brand identity, print materials, and digital design. My work consistently focuses on creating thoughtful, results-driven solutions that support both visual clarity and business objectives. In addition to my professional practice, I have applied my expertise as an educator, helping students throughout the region develop a strong foundation in design and creative thinking.

I am always interested in new opportunities and creative challenges. If you would like to collaborate, please feel free to reach out via email or through the contact form at the bottom of this page.

Services

What I offer

Logo & Identity Design

I create custom logos that are clear, versatile, and built to last. Each logo is thoughtfully designed to reflect your brand’s identity and work seamlessly across print and digital platforms.

Print Design

I design high-quality print materials that are clear, consistent, and production-ready. From flyers and brochures to business cards and signage, every piece is crafted to represent your brand well and print beautifully.

WordPress Web Design

I design custom WordPress websites that are responsive, easy to manage, and built around your brand. Every site is thoughtfully crafted for performance, usability, and long-term growth—so your website works for you, not against you.
